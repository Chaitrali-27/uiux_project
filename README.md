## Overview
A comprehensive redesign of the official Government of India **Passport Seva Portal**, resolving 15 visual/interface (UI) flaws and 15 interaction/experience (UX) flaws identified in Assignment 01.

## Branches & Workflow
- `main`: Production-ready integrated portal.
- `ui`: Implementation and resolution of 15 UI flaws (UI-01 to UI-15).
- `ux`: Implementation and resolution of 15 UX flaws (UX-01 to UX-15).
